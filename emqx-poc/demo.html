Why sticky LB: an MQTT 5 cleanStart=false client that reconnects to a different node has to fetch its session cross-cluster (slow) or silently starts fresh (lost queued messages). balance source keeps a client on its node.

POC 02 Live: the connection storm (thundering herd)

A broker restart drops every client at once. If they all reconnect with the same timing, the surviving broker collapses under the wave. Pick a reconnect strategy, hit Restart broker, and watch the accept-rate. Decorrelated jitter spreads the herd; the others don't.
